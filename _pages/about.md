I am a Ph.D. student in Mechanical Engineering at the University of California, Los Angeles. I work in the [PRACTICE Lab](https://practice-lab-ucla.github.io/) with [Prof. Anushri Dixit](https://practice-lab-ucla.github.io/people/anushri_dixit/).

My research is about helping robots explore new places safely. Vision-language models give a robot a high-level understanding of what it sees, but they are often overconfident. I use conformal prediction to measure how much the robot should trust each decision, with statistical guarantees. The goal is a robot that knows when it is unsure and acts carefully. This work led to [CPNAV]({{ '/projects/cpnav/' | relative_url }}), a calibrated navigation framework tested in simulation and on a real robot.

Before UCLA, I earned an M.S. in Mechanical Engineering at Carnegie Mellon University. There I worked in the [AirLab](https://theairlab.org/) with Prof. Sebastian Scherer and Prof. Junyi Geng on a unified model predictive controller for a [tilt-rotor VTOL aircraft]({{ '/projects/vtol/' | relative_url }}). I earned my B.S. in Mechanical Engineering at the University of California, Irvine.

In summer 2026 I was a research intern at [FieldAI](https://www.fieldai.com/).

I am interested in robotics, safe decision-making with AI, uncertainty quantification, and control.
