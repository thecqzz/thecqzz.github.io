**Main contributor**, Carnegie Mellon University · January to June 2023 · Advisors: Prof. Zeynep Temel and Prof. Cameron Riviere

Stairs are hard for cleaning robots. We designed and built a robot that climbs stairs and cleans them on its own, and it beat similar robots from previous years on overall task success rate.

- Designed a durable chassis and cleaning mechanism that works on different stair surfaces.
- Integrated ultrasonic range finders, infrared sensors, and a camera for obstacle detection and navigation.
- Wrote the control software on ROS 1, including path planning, obstacle avoidance, and a state machine for switching operating modes.
- Presented the robot publicly at CMU TechSpark and to several industrial robotics companies.
