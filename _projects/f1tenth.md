**Main contributor**, Carnegie Mellon University · August to December 2023 · Advisor: Prof. John M. Dolan

F1TENTH is a 1/10 scale autonomous race car. The goal is to drive fast around a track, avoid obstacles, and overtake other cars. I built the driving stack on ROS 2 and tested it both in simulation and on the real car.

- Implemented obstacle avoidance, RRT\* path planning, model predictive control (MPC), and model predictive path integral (MPPI) control.
- Developed a dynamic emergency avoidance algorithm that reacts in time at high speed.
- Wrote a pure-pursuit controller for accurate trajectory tracking and map visualization.
