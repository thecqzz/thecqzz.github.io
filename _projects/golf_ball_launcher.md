**Team of four**, University of California, Irvine

We designed and modeled a semi-autonomous golf ball launcher that lets the user set a target distance and hit it consistently. A MATLAB model computes the firing angle from the launch force and trajectory before each shot. After several rounds of testing and tuning, the launcher delivers balls with repeatable accuracy.
