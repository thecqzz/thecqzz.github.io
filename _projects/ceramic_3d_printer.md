**Research intern**, Electroactive Materials Characterization Lab, Pennsylvania State University · June to August 2021 · Advisors: Prof. Zoubeida Ounaie and Prof. Amrita Basak

Ceramic 3D printing is useful but usually expensive, and the extruders leak and clog. I worked on a printer that is cheaper and more reliable.

- Studied how nozzle speed, nozzle height, and extrusion speed affect biopolymer printing quality.
- Studied barium titanate and designed a printer that can handle ceramic paste.
- Reduced leaks with rubber sealing and a one-part, screwless extruder design, which also improved material flow.
