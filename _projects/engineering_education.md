**Student researcher**, Prof. Natascha Buswell's research group, University of California, Irvine · March to September 2022

New faculty often say they feel unprepared for teaching. This project asked why. I analyzed 12 interview transcripts with new mechanical engineering professors using ATLAS.ti, a qualitative data analysis tool. I coded the transcripts, grouped the codes into themes, and compared the shared challenges with the individual differences.

The results point to specific gaps in how doctoral programs prepare future faculty, and they were meant to inform faculty development programs.
