**Main contributor**, University of California, Los Angeles · April to June 2025 · Advisor: Prof. Veronica Santos

Giving an injection needs the right depth and the right force, and skin stiffness varies from person to person. I designed a robot arm system for precise and safe skin injection, with a focus on mechanical safety and a user-centered design.

- Built a centralized impedance controller that regulates the contact force during the injection.
- Verified the injection process in a custom MATLAB simulation across a range of skin stiffness values and penetration depths.
