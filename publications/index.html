  1. Preprint
    CPNAV: Calibrated Vision-Language Navigation for Object-Goal Search in Unseen Environments
    Qizhao Chen, Yuanhong Zeng, Shoh Nishino, and Anushri Dixit
    In Preprint, 2026
    Abs Bib PDF Website

    Directing a robot to locate a target object in unseen environments remains a central challenge in embodied AI. Vision-Language Model (VLM)-based methods provide rich semantic reasoning but often produce uncalibrated and overconfident action decisions. We introduce CPNAV, a navigation framework that combines collision-aware planning with statistically calibrated VLM action scoring for map-free object-goal navigation. From RGB-D input, CPNAV constructs a navigability map, generates collision-free motion primitives, and queries two VLMs for goal detection and action scoring. The resulting scores are calibrated offline via conformal prediction (CP) using oracle actions obtained through a tree-structured exploration procedure with backtracking, providing finite-sample guarantees at a user-specified risk level. The CP layer is model-agnostic and can wrap any VLM policy. Online, calibrated VLM scores guide action selection, while a tree-structured exploration history records alternative actions and allows backtracking. We evaluate CPNAV in both simulation and real-world hardware settings. In AI Habitat simulation, compared with the uncalibrated baseline method, CPNAV achieves relative SR and SPL improvements of 6.2% and 11.4% on HM3D v0.2, and 12.4% and 8.8% on MP3D, respectively. In real-world deployment on a modified Hiwonder MentorPi robot, the corresponding improvements in SR and SPL are 24.9% and 15.2%, respectively.
